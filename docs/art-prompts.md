## Final prompt set

### Room

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background plate for a playable isometric life simulation game, landscape 16:10. Create a premium, near-photorealistic 3D rendered cutaway studio room with realistic PBR materials, physically plausible afternoon sunlight, ambient occlusion, rich contact shadows, cinematic clarity, architectural visualization quality, tactile honey oak parquet boards, cream plaster walls, dusty muted sage accents, timber skirting and architectural trim. Orthographic isometric camera, two back walls, open front, camera looking diagonally down into the room. The diamond floor must occupy normalized vertices: back (50%,24%), right (88%,55%), front (50%,86%), left (12%,55%). Two walls rise behind the back edges; top back corner near (50%,4%). Entire diorama fits with margins on warm desaturated taupe background. Large realistic white-framed window on right back wall, soft cream linen curtains, leafy tree view, long angled sunlight and window shadows on wooden floor. Left wall: framed botanical print and slim floating shelf with books. Floor empty of furniture, residents and rugs for separate interactive sprites. Wood, textured plaster, no cartoon outlines or flat vector shapes. No text, labels, grids, people, characters, watermark or UI. High budget realistic cozy 3D video game interior.
